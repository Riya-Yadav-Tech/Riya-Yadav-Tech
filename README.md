### 📦 Featured Projects

#### 🚚 [DataCo Supply Chain Analytics](https://github.com/Riya-Yadav-Tech/DataCo-Supply-Chain-Analytics)
`SQL Server` `Power BI` `DAX` `Excel`
- Analyzed **65.75K orders** ($36.78M revenue) and built **4 Power BI dashboards** for delivery, market, customer and profitability analysis
- Found that **54.82% of orders were delivered late** and identified the highest-risk regions (**56–58% late rate**) and shipping modes

#### ⏱️ [Olist Late-Delivery Early Warning](https://github.com/Riya-Yadav-Tech/olist-late-delivery-early-warning) · [🔗 Live Demo](https://olist-late-delivery-riya.streamlit.app)
`Python` `Pandas` `scikit-learn` `Streamlit`
- Built a model that predicts late-delivery risk **at the moment an order is placed**, trained on **96K orders** with a time-based split to avoid data leakage
- The riskiest 10% of orders were late **2.6× more often** than average and held **25.9% of all late deliveries**
