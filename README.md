### 📦 Featured Projects

#### 🏪 [Quick-Commerce Dark-Store Demand Forecasting](https://github.com/Riya-Yadav-Tech/quick-commerce-demand-forecasting) · [🔗 Live Demo](https://qcommerce-demand-riya.streamlit.app)
`Python` `Pandas` `LightGBM` `Streamlit`
- Forecast daily demand **one week ahead** for **54 stores × 33 product categories** (2.7M rows) to plan Blinkit / Zepto-style dark-store stock
- Used only information available 7 days in advance, with a time-based split and a **locked 28-day test set** to avoid data leakage
- Cut forecast error by **32.5%** vs a "same as last week" baseline (WAPE **12.5% vs 18.5%**), reducing **overstock units by 43.7%** and **understock units by 19.3%**

#### ⏱️ [Olist Late-Delivery Early Warning](https://github.com/Riya-Yadav-Tech/olist-late-delivery-early-warning) · [🔗 Live Demo](https://olist-late-delivery-riya.streamlit.app)
`Python` `Pandas` `scikit-learn` `LightGBM` `Streamlit`
- Predicts late-delivery risk **at the moment an order is placed**, using 9 joined tables and **96K delivered orders** with a time-based split
- The riskiest 10% of orders were late **2.6× more often** than average and held **25.9% of all late deliveries**
- Streamlit app with **Low / Medium / High risk bands** so an operations team can act on the riskiest orders first

#### 🚚 [DataCo Supply Chain Analytics](https://github.com/Riya-Yadav-Tech/DataCo-Supply-Chain-Analytics)
`SQL Server` `Power BI` `DAX` `Excel`
- Analyzed **65.75K orders** ($36.78M revenue) and built **4 Power BI dashboards** for delivery, market, customer and profitability analysis
- Found that **54.82% of orders were delivered late**, flagged the highest-risk regions (**56–58% late rate**), and recommended judging shipping modes by **late-delivery rate, not late-order count**
